## What a civil scope looks like with us

Most of our civil work is the ground package for a telecommunications site: the foundation, the slab, the trench and the track that gets plant to the compound. Civil and road-access crews work alongside our rigging crews. One contractor can take a greenfield site from clearing to the pour and on to the tower build.

### Foundations and piers

Bored piers are drilled in-house. Our auger set runs from 600 mm to 2100 mm, which covers monopole and lattice tower pads through to broadcast mast foundations. Where the ground turns to rock, the 12 t excavator carries a rock breaker, so drilling does not stop while a breaker is hired in. Reinforcing cages, anchor-bolt cages and hold-down bolts are set to the engineer's drawings before the pour.

### Slabs, huts and ODUs

Equipment slabs, ODU slabs and hut footings on greenfield and existing sites. Slab swap-outs on live sites are planned around the carrier's outage window, with our riggers handling the equipment moves.

### Trenching, conduit and earthing

Cable trenches by trencher or excavator, conduit, pit and riser installation, earthing, and underground cable runs into the compound. Aerial cable runs and feeder work are covered under [comms and rigging](/services/comms-and-rigging/).

### Access and site preparation

Access tracks and crossovers, hardstand for EWPs and cranes, compound preparation, and the tipper and float to move our own plant in and out. On rooftop sites we re-sheet and seal around new installations, so the building owner is left with a weatherproof roof.

### Plant

Every item of civil plant we use is our own. The small end is the 3 t and 5 t excavators, skid steer and trencher for tight sites. The large end is the 12 t excavator with rock breaker, the crane trucks and the 6x6 tipper and float. The full list is on the [generators and plant page](/fleet/generators-and-plant/), and the same plant is available for dry hire under our [hire terms](/hire-terms/).

### How we price and report

Civil scopes are priced from your design pack and any geotechnical report. Each item of plant has its own SWMS, every site gets a risk assessment, and we close out with as-built photos and the records your specification requires. Ask for the [prequalification pack](/compliance/) if procurement needs insurances, licences and policies before award.
