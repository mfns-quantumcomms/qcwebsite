## What a comms and rigging scope looks like with us

We take a site from permits to handover. Rigging crews are led by advanced riggers with tower-rescue, working-at-heights and RadHaz currency, and every team leader's vehicle carries the safety kit listed on our compliance page. Where a site has no crane access we erect with gin poles.

### Typical scopes

- **Greenfield builds.** Full eJV, LTE and L700 greenfield builds including civils, tower or pole erection, shelter, cabling, antenna systems and commissioning.
- **Upgrades and swap-outs.** Head frame swap-outs, antenna and RRU replacement, feeder and fibre changes on live sites, with carrier outage windows managed.
- **Structural.** Mast and tower restrengthening, painting and camouflaging, LadSaf installation and inspection.
- **Testing and commissioning.** PIM (720 to 757 MHz), sweep, fibre and BER testing with full handover documentation.
- **Decommissioning.** Full site decommissions including make-good.

### How we work with carriers and tier ones

Scopes are priced from your design pack. We supply SWMS, lift plans and rescue plans per site, report through your portal or ours, and close out with as-built photos and test results. Ask for the prequalification pack if your procurement team needs insurances, licences and policies up front.
