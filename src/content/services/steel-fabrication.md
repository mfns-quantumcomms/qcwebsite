## What we fabricate

Telecommunications steel is rarely off the shelf. A head frame swap-out needs a frame that matches the pole top and the antenna layout. A restrengthening job needs a jacket cut to the pole's taper. We set up fabrication at Bylands to bring that steel in-house for our own crews, and we now fabricate for clients as well.

### Typical items

- **Head frames and mounts.** Antenna head frames, RRU and dish mounts, standoff brackets and rooftop frames, built to the design pack.
- **Monopole jackets and strengthening steel.** Split jackets, flange plates and stiffeners for mast and tower restrengthening.
- **Cages.** Reinforcing cages and anchor-bolt cages for pier and pad foundations, tied to the engineer's schedule and delivered with the civil crew.
- **Frames and supports.** Solar array frames, equipment frames, cable ladder and tray supports, hut and shelter skids.
- **Plates and sets.** Base plates, gussets, cleats and bolt sets cut and folded to drawing.

### Workshop process

Plate and section are cut on the CNC plasma cutter, folded on the brake press and welded into assemblies. Finished items go to [thermal coating](/services/thermal-coating/) or out to a galvaniser, then to site or into stock.

### Lead times and stock

Standard brackets, mounts and plates are held in stock, so common items do not wait on a fabrication slot. Custom items are quoted with a lead time against the drawing. Coating is usually the longest step in a steel programme, which is why the coating line sits in the same workshop.

### Supply-only or installed

Most steel from Bylands is installed by our own riggers, so a fit issue on the pole goes straight back to the person who cut it. Supply-only is also fine. Send drawings, quantities and a delivery address, and we quote fabricated, coated and delivered.
